## About Me

I'm a software engineer based in Canada with 4+ years of professional experience building and owning production applications.

My work goes beyond implementing individual features. I've taken systems from architecture and development through deployment, infrastructure, integrations, troubleshooting, and ongoing production support.

A large part of my experience sits at the intersection of **full-stack development, backend engineering, cloud infrastructure, payments, and real-world devices**.

### What I work on

- Architecting and developing full-stack applications with **TypeScript, Node.js, NestJS, Angular, and React**
- Building backend services around **payments, transactional workflows, authentication, APIs, and asynchronous processing**
- Designing and maintaining systems using **MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, AWS, Docker, Nginx, and Linux**
- Building event-driven integrations using **AWS SQS**
- Integrating payment platforms, commerce APIs, authentication providers, and other third-party services
- Connecting web applications with physical hardware including printers, payment terminals, vending controllers, cash-handling devices, and serial-connected peripherals
- Owning production deployments, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, incident investigation, and reliability improvements
